# My Health privacy policy

My Health is published under the brand RockyK Technologies. For privacy questions contact peter.njengakihoro@gmail.com. Effective date: October 16, 2026.

My Health is a personal wellness journal, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, prevent, cure or treat medical conditions. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

## Information on your device

The app stores the profile name you choose, goals, step history, journal entries, medication reminders, imported Health Connect records and saved GPS routes in Android's private app storage. The developer does not receive these records and operates no advertising or health-data analytics service. No My Health user account is required.

Physical activity access is used to count steps automatically in the background and organize them by local calendar day. Location is used during a GPS workout you start, including while the screen is off. The app does not request background location permission. Camera frames are used locally to decode product barcodes and are not uploaded or saved by My Health. Android handles fingerprint/device-credential authentication; My Health does not access or store biometric templates.

Health Connect access is optional and read-only. The app imports the past 30 days of approved steps, sleep, exercise, weight, heart rate, blood pressure, blood glucose and temperature into your local journal. It refreshes on demand and when you return to the app, up to every 15 minutes. Health Connect records are not used for advertising, sold, uploaded to the developer or written back. You can revoke individual permissions in Android settings.

## Optional product lookup

When you scan or enter a food barcode, the app sends that barcode over HTTPS to Open Food Facts to retrieve nutrition information. The service necessarily receives standard connection metadata, including the IP address and the application's User-Agent. The request includes no profile name, journal, health measurements, purchase data or GPS coordinates. The app caches looked-up product information on your device for future offline use. Open Food Facts operates its service under its own privacy practices; see https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

## Google Play purchases

The optional one-time Pro upgrade uses Google Play Billing. Google handles payment methods, purchase processing and its associated account information, and may process technical and transaction information needed to operate the payment service. My Health does not receive your payment card or bank details. The app receives and locally verifies signed purchase data to grant access, acknowledge completed purchases and restore purchases. Locally cached receipts allow offline access; the app checks current purchases when Google Play is available. No purchase receipt is sent to a developer-operated server in this version. Google processes its payment and technical information under its own privacy terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## Exports, retention and deletion

Health records remain on your device until you delete entries, choose Delete all health data, clear Android app storage or uninstall. Automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. Explicit JSON/CSV/PDF exports are saved to the location you choose and may contain sensitive health data. The app does not control what a chosen cloud document provider does with an exported file. Deleting app records does not delete exported copies or source records in Health Connect.

Delete all health data removes the local journal, steps, routes, profile, food cache and reminder schedules, while preserving the Pro purchase and Android permissions. Revoke activity permission in Android settings to stop further step collection. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove all app-local data, including cached purchase receipts; eligible Pro purchases can be restored through Google Play.

There are no My Health server-side accounts or health records to delete. Contact peter.njengakihoro@gmail.com for privacy questions; do not send sensitive health records unless necessary for a support request.

## Security and changes

Android app-private storage protects local files; app lock can additionally restrict screen access. HTTPS protects product-lookup traffic. No system is guaranteed secure. We will update this policy when app behavior changes and publish the revised effective date.
