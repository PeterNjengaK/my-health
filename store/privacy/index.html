Who provides My Health

My Health is published by an individual developer under the brand RockyK Technologies. Privacy and support contact: peter.njengakihoro@gmail.com.

My Health is a personal wellness journal, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, prevent, cure or treat medical conditions. Steps, distance and walking calories may be estimates. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

Records on your device

The app stores the profile name you enter, unit preferences, goals, daily step history, journal entries, medication and hydration reminders, imported Health Connect records, saved GPS routes and cached food information in Android’s private app storage.

The developer does not receive these health records, sell them, or operate a health-data advertising or analytics service. No My Health user account is required.

Device permissions

  • Physical activity: counts steps automatically in the background and organizes them by local calendar day. Android permissions, force-stop and battery restrictions can affect recording.
  • Notifications: display tracking progress, workout status and reminders you enable. Android may delay reminders to save battery.
  • Location: records a GPS route only during a workout you start, including while the screen is off. The app does not request Android’s background-location permission. Routes stay on this phone.
  • Camera: decodes food product barcodes locally. My Health does not save or upload camera frames.
  • Device authentication: optional app lock uses Android’s fingerprint or device-credential authentication. My Health does not receive or store biometric templates.

You can manage permissions in Android Settings. Denying an optional permission affects its associated feature.

Optional Health Connect access

On supported Android devices, you can choose read-only access to steps, sleep, exercise sessions, weight, heart rate, blood pressure, blood glucose and body temperature. The app imports approved records from the last 30 days into your local journal, refreshing on request and when you return, up to every 15 minutes.

Health Connect data is used to display your personal health history and trends. It is not sold, used for advertising, uploaded to the developer, or written back to Health Connect. Revoke access in Android’s Health Connect settings. Deleting an imported journal entry does not delete its source record in Health Connect.

Optional food barcode lookup

When you scan or enter a product barcode, My Health sends that barcode over HTTPS to Open Food Facts to retrieve nutrition information. Open Food Facts receives ordinary connection information, including your IP address and the app’s User-Agent.

The request includes no profile name, journal entries, health measurements, purchase information or GPS coordinates. Results are cached on the device for later use. Open Food Facts is community-maintained; check nutrition values against the package label. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy.

Google Play purchases

My Health Pro is an optional one-time upgrade, not a subscription. Google Play handles payment methods, transaction processing and associated account information. My Health does not receive your payment card or bank details.

The app receives and locally verifies signed purchase data to unlock Pro, acknowledge completed purchases and restore ownership. Purchase receipts are cached locally for offline access; current purchases are checked when Google Play is available. This version sends no receipt to a developer-operated server.

Google and its service components may process technical, account and transaction information needed to operate Google Play Billing. See Google’s privacy policy.

Retention, exports & deletion

Health records remain on your device until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall. Automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. Keep a manual backup before uninstalling or changing phones.

  • Delete one entry: use the journal’s delete control.
  • Delete all health data: in the You screen, this removes the local journal, steps, routes, profile, food cache and reminder schedules. It preserves the Pro purchase and Android permissions.
  • Stop further step collection: revoke activity recognition in Android Settings. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes app-local data, including cached purchase receipts; eligible purchases can be restored through Google Play.
  • Exports: JSON backups and CSV/PDF exports go to the location or document provider you choose. They can contain sensitive health information. The selected storage provider’s practices apply. Deleting app records does not delete exported files or source Health Connect records.

There are no My Health server-side accounts or health records to delete. For privacy questions, contact peter.njengakihoro@gmail.com.

Security & policy changes

Android’s app-private storage protects local files. Optional app lock restricts access to app screens. HTTPS protects food-lookup traffic. No device or service can be guaranteed secure; protect your device and any exported backups.

We will update this policy when app behavior changes and publish the revised effective date on this page.
