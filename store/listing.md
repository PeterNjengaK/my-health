# Google Play listing draft

App name: **My Health**

Short description:

Track daily steps and your health habits. Ad-free, with optional one-time Pro.

Full description:

Make room for a healthier everyday with My Health: a calm, ad-free step counter and personal wellness journal.

DAILY STEPS, AUTOMATICALLY

Allow physical activity access once. My Health then counts steps while your phone is with you, including in the background. Each day starts fresh at 00:00 using your phone's local time. Your previous totals stay in history. Set a daily goal, see your progress, and add a home-screen steps widget.

YOUR HEALTH JOURNAL

Log water, sleep, weight, meals, workouts, mood, symptoms, medication doses, cycle notes and measured vital signs. Edit past entries and follow your habits over time. Daily medication and hydration reminders help you remember the small things.

CONNECTED HEALTH

On Android 14 and later, choose read-only Health Connect access to import supported activity, sleep and measurements from compatible apps and wearables. My Health does not directly pair a watch or independently measure sleep or vital signs.

YOUR DATA, YOUR PHONE

Health records and GPS routes stay in private app storage. There are no ads or analytics. Optional app locking uses Android biometrics or your device credential. JSON backups and CSV exports remain available without Pro. Choose where exports are stored.

ONE-TIME PRO UPGRADE

Unlock GPS walks, runs and rides with route previews; food barcode scanning and portion logging; 30- and 90-day movement calendars and trends; and printable PDF health reports. Buy Pro once through Google Play: no recurring subscription. Purchase availability and price are displayed by Google Play.

Barcode lookup sends only a product barcode to Open Food Facts. Community nutrition data may be incomplete; compare it with the package label.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

My Health is a personal wellness journal, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, prevent, cure or treat any condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice. Blood pressure, blood glucose, temperature and heart rate require an appropriate separate measuring device or a compatible data source.

A supported step-counter sensor is needed for automatic steps. Walking distance from steps is an estimate; GPS workouts depend on signal quality. Android may delay reminders or stop background work. Force-stopping the app pauses tracking until you reopen it. GPS workouts recover paused after an interruption. Past steps from before the initial sensor baseline cannot be recovered.

Suggested category: Health & Fitness. Contains ads: No. Contains in-app purchases: Yes.
